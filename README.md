# How AI Is Changing Student Life in India

## Data Analysis Report

### Exploratory Data Analysis on AI Adoption Among Indian Students

**Dataset:** AI Student Life India 2026.csv
**Records:** 5,000 students
**Scope:** Age, gender, education level, city, AI tool, usage hours, purpose, grade impact, satisfaction
**Date:** September 26, 2026
**Author:** Ayyam Ullah

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project examines how **Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools are being used by Indian students** based on a survey-style dataset containing **5,000 student records** from school, college, and university levels across **29 Indian cities**.

The analysis focuses on:

* Student demographics
* AI tool preferences
* Daily AI usage
* Purpose of AI usage
* Reported impact on grades
* Student satisfaction
* City-wise AI adoption
* Gender distribution across AI tools

The main objective is to understand how widespread and varied AI usage is among students and whether there is any discernible relationship between AI usage and reported academic or satisfaction outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Business Question

> **How widespread and varied is AI tool usage among Indian students, and is there any discernible relationship between how much AI students use and their reported academic and satisfaction outcomes?**

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **5,000 student records** with no missing values.

It includes the following fields:

| Field              | Description                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Student ID         | Unique student identifier                  |
| Age                | Student age, ranging from 15–25            |
| Gender             | Student gender                             |
| Education Level    | School, College, or University             |
| City               | Student's city                             |
| AI Tool Used       | AI platform used by the student            |
| Daily Usage Hours  | Daily AI usage, ranging from 0.5–6.5 hours |
| Purpose            | Main purpose of AI usage                   |
| Impact on Grades   | Reported academic impact                   |
| Satisfaction Level | Student satisfaction with AI tools         |

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis aims to:

1. Establish the demographic composition of the surveyed students.
2. Identify the most widely used AI tools and their purposes.
3. Characterize daily AI usage intensity.
4. Examine usage patterns across education levels.
5. Examine whether usage intensity relates to reported grade impact or satisfaction.
6. Identify cities with the highest AI adoption.

Students in the dataset range from **15 to 25 years old**.

## 2. Daily AI Usage Patterns

Daily AI usage ranges from **0.5 to 6.5 hours**.

## 3. Gender Distribution

The dataset contains a nearly balanced gender distribution:

* **Male:** 2,428 students — 48.6%

This represents a relatively even split between the two groups.

## 4. Education Level

Students are almost evenly distributed across the three education levels:

* **School:** 1,674
* **University:** 1,665
* **College:** 1,661

No single education level dominates the dataset.

---

## 5. AI Tool Adoption

Seven AI tools are included in the analysis:

* Copilot
* Gemini
* Notion AI
* Perplexity
* ChatGPT
* Grammarly
* Claude

Usage of these tools is relatively comparable, with approximately **696–735 students** using each tool.

This indicates that AI adoption is broad-based rather than concentrated around a single platform.

## 7. Impact on Grades

Reported grade impact is almost evenly divided:

| Grade Impact   | Students |
| -------------- | -------: |
| Slight Decline |    1,675 |
| No Change      |    1,669 |
| Improved       |    1,656 |

There is no dominant outcome in the dataset.

The analysis also shows that average AI usage hours are very similar across the three grade-impact groups, ranging from approximately **3.41 to 3.56 hours per day**.

Therefore, within this dataset, usage intensity alone does not clearly predict reported grade outcomes.

# 🔎 Key Insights

### 1. Balanced Demographics

The sample is close to evenly split by gender, with **51.4% female and 48.6% male** students.

School, College, and University students are also represented in approximately equal proportions.

### 2. Even Age Distribution

Students aged **15–25** are represented in relatively similar numbers, with no single age group standing out.

### 3. No Dominant AI Tool

Adoption is spread relatively evenly across the seven AI tools analyzed.

### 4. Broad-Based AI Usage

Writing, Learning, Research, Homework, and Coding are all common purposes of AI usage.

### 5. Moderate Average Usage

Students use AI for approximately **3.5 hours per day on average**.

Usage ranges from **0.5 to 6.5 hours per day**.

### 6. Mixed Grade Impact

Reported grade impact is almost equally divided between:

* Improved
* No Change
* Slight Decline

Average usage hours are also very similar across these groups, ranging from **3.41–3.56 hours**.

### 7. Even Satisfaction Levels

Low, Medium, and High satisfaction levels are almost equally represented.

### 8. AI Adoption Across Cities

Although Lucknow, Jaipur, Mumbai, Vijayawada, and Pune have the highest raw student counts, the differences between cities are relatively modest.

### 9. Important Data Limitation

The report notes that the **near-uniform distribution across many categorical and numerical fields suggests that the dataset may be synthetically generated rather than based on organic survey responses**.

Therefore, the dataset is useful for practicing:

* Exploratory Data Analysis
* Data Visualization
* Statistical analysis
* Dashboard development
* Data interpretation

However, conclusions about real-world causal relationships, such as whether increased AI usage causes changes in grades, should be interpreted cautiously.
